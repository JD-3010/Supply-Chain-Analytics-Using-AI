
## 1. Basic KPIs for Supply Chain

- Total Order Lines
- Line Fill Rates
- Volume Fill Rate
- Total Orders
- On time delivery %
- In Time Delivery %
- On Time in Full %

- So what are these.,

### Total Order Lines:

- Suppose let's say a person orders a product (say 1 keyboard) today  and that is entered in the sheet
- The very next day he order 2 different items (a keyboard and a mouse together) 
- So totally he has Ordered Twice ---> So Orders = 2
- But in Sheet the data for each product will be in separate rows or lines ---> Order Lines = 3
- ![[Pasted image 20261005115944.png]]

- **Orders are nothing but a unique request placed by a customer on a given date** 
- **Within an order, a customer could request multiple items. Each of these items requested within the order is called an order line** 
- **Example: Let's say you order 4 notebooks and 2 pens at Amazon. A unique order ID is generated for all these items. Notebook and Pen is an order line.**

### Line Fill Rate VS Volume Fill Rate:

- So Let's say we have another column saying whether the item were delivered in full or not.
- Like if the person order 5 keyboard and the 5 keyboards delivered ---> then it is a Yes (or 1 in Binary)
- Likewise if it was delivered in full ---> then it is No (or 0 in Binary)
- So lets say we have 3 Order lines out of which ... 2 is Yes and 1 is No .
- So how many lines we have yes 2 ---> **So the Line fill rate will be 2/3 ==> 
- ==**Line Fill Rate = Number of Full Delivered / Total Order Lines==**

- So for Volume Fill Rate ... Instead of Lines we will take the Quantity that has been delivered in full or not 
	So for example the Total Quantity Order = 20 ; Quantity Delivered = 17
- **==Volume Fill Rate = No. Quantity Delivered / Total Quantity Ordered==**
![[Pasted image 20261005121013.png]]
- **These 2 are very Important Metric for Supply Planners & Supply Managers or Production Managers **

- Line Fill Rate is an important metric for the supply planning team to understand how many lines they shipped out of the total lines ordered. This metric does not consider the delivery time of the order. 
- Volume fill rate or case fill rate is a similar metric useful for the supply planning team to understand the total quantity they are able to ship for a customer per order or for a given period of time

### On Time & Info 

- Let's Expand the data table further more as **Agreed Delivery Date and Actual Delivery Date** & Another Column saying "Delivered on Time ?'"
- In Full Order (IF) measure is measured at the order level. It determines if an order is delivered in full as per the requested quantity by the customer.
- An order is In Full only when all the line items inside the order are delivered In Full.

- **NOTE : In Full Order (IF) --> Is calculated by Orders and not by Order line ... Suppose the person has ordered ==only twice== the 2nd Order has 4 items so the order line will be 5                    Order 1 = 1 ; Order 2 = 4 , and Order 2 was not delivered in full quantity in some product. so the ==In Full Order value will be only 1 and not 3== excluding the not fully delivered Product  **

- In terms of On time Orders ---> **Even tho the Quantity was not was not delivered in full as requested the Order was Delivered on time so the On Time Orders(OT) will be 2**.
- On Time Orders (OT) this measure is measured at the order level. It determines if an order is delivered as per the agreed time with the customer.
- An order is On Time only when all the line items inside the order is delivered on time

![[Pasted image 20261005122131.png]]

- These are many used by Warehouse and Distribution people 

### On Time In Time Percentage (OTIF) (VERY IMPORTANT):

- OTIF this measure is measured at the order level. It determines if an order is delivered BOTH in full and On Time as per the customer order request.
- This metric is important for all the sub functions in the supply chain team.
- An order is OTIF only when all the line items inside the order are delivered In Full and ON Time. This is a hard metric which measures the reliability of an order from customer's point of view.
- **==Tip : Use a Consolidated Data that is based on Orders and not on separate Order lines==**
- Let's take the previous example it self .... Order 1 is Delivered in full and on time ---> So yes
- But the Order 2 was in time but one of the product was not delivered in full ----> so no 
- Suppose lets say a Person orders 500 Different products in Different Quantities ... 499 Products where delivered in full and on time but just one product could not be delivered in full but it was on time ...Still the Value will go to 0 and It Will loose the OTIF value . That is why this metric is called very harsh metric
- So find the percentage of In Full and On Time values 
- **==So On Time In Full (OTIF) Value will be ==
- **==Total No. of Orders that where delivered On time and In Full / Total No. of Orders==**
