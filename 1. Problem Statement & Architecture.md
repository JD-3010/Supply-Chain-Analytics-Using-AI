Atliq is a Gujarat based organic food manufacturer. They specialize only in few products but disrupted the market in the two cities they operate. In an interesting move, they opened their third city of operation in New Jersey, America, and started performing well there as well. Though they handle very less products, their supply chain is immature. Bruce Hurali, the chief
operating officer of Atlique Mart, has noticed that some of their supermarket customers are dissatisfied with their order management and he wanted to fix this before scaling further. Bruce discussed this with Tony Sharma, the head of analytics at Atlake Mart and understood the problem is a classic supply chain issue where they are not able to maintain an optimum inventory. Tony offered to create a quick insights report using Excel or PowerBI, but Bruce insisted that he needs something AI powered as a solution because like any other senior leaders in the world, Bruce does not want to miss out on the AI hype. Tony assigned this work to Peter Pande who is a curious data guy in the team exploring all the AI tools in the market. Peter, who always thrives to drive the extra mile, found quadratic, which is an AI powered spreadsheet that even offers connection to their database in Postgra DB. He even figured out an automation using N8N to migrate the data directly from emails to Postgres. So you will be solving the supply chain problem along with Bruce, Tony and Peter using Quadratic, the AI powered spreadsheet N8N to automate the data migration and learn important supply chain domain experts. ## Architecture of the Stack / Process:

- The Data will be coming through emails (It can be of different sources but in this case let's say the data comes in through emails)

- Then is Goes to Postgres (an Database management tool)
- Then into **Quadratic Tool** , it is an AI Spreadsheet . And here the prompt based analysis will be done.
